A century after us.

The last buses cross the square. Mortar opens along the old brickworks. A corner peels away from the tower, and its walls come to rest in the street. Wind and birds keep moving.

Scrub the year to follow a fracture in either direction. Ferns take root on exposed floors after the rubble settles. Drag sideways to orbit, down to raise the camera, or up to lower it. Scroll or pinch to zoom; Shift + drag to pan. With the scene focused, use the arrow keys, +/− and Home.
